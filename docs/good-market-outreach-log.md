## Platform Profile & Partnership Research (2026-08-04)

Reviewed Good Market's own Info subsite (goodmarket.global/info) — About, Digital Commons, Networks, Spotlight, Community Rules — to find ways to raise Velor's profile and find mission-aligned partners beyond one-to-one outreach. Findings:

- Velor already holds the "Good Market Approved" badge on its Enterprise Account; further paid verifications (WFTO, B Corp, etc.) remain deferred pending budget, per the existing note above.
- Good Market's "Networks" feature (goodmarket.global/info/networks) lets any enterprise with a network of other enterprises create a free Network page, listed alongside established networks like MADE51, WFTO, Fair Trade Federation, Freedom Business Alliance, Thistle Farms, and Global Goods Partners. Not actionable yet — this becomes available once Velor has onboarded a meaningful number of Founding Sellers, and should be revisited at that milestone.
- Good Market's "Community Groups" feature (goodmarket.global/groups) offers Matrix-based chat/forums/polls; existing groups are mostly regional. A "Create New Group" option exists for future use once Velor has real sellers to bring into a group.
- Good Market's own stated philosophy (Digital Commons / About pages) explicitly encourages off-platform trade and direct connections between buyers and enterprises ("Think Ecosystem" rather than a closed platform) — this confirms sending outreach candidates to Velor's own homepage is aligned with, not contrary to, Good Market's values.
- New lead-sourcing technique discovered: searching Good Market's own directory by the name of a known mission-aligned network (e.g. "MADE51") surfaces member organizations directly, rather than relying only on geographic/region searches. This produced the 8-organization batch below in a single search and should be reused for other named networks (WFTO, Fair Trade Federation, Freedom Business Alliance, Thistle Farms, Global Goods Partners, etc.) in future sessions.
