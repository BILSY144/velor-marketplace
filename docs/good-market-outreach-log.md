Listing description (as published):

Velor is a global marketplace built exclusively for real makers: traditional artisans, family workshops, and craft cooperatives. Factory, wholesale, and dropship sellers are excluded, so buyers know every piece is genuinely handmade with real origin and maker attribution. Our mission is to help artisans and handcraft makers build a real, sustainable living from their craft, including people rebuilding their lives after homelessness. We are recruiting Founding Sellers now. Verified makers get a free, permanent Founding Seller badge, priority placement, free self-serve listings, escrow protection with payouts released on confirmed delivery, and the ability to live-broadcast their craft in their own language so buyers can watch it made and ask questions in real time. Sellers always set their own prices and keep full control of their work. We are especially keen to hear from social enterprises and makers who support people rebuilding their lives, including those affected by homelessness — join us and turn your craft into a livelihood. Message us here or visit velorcommerce.store/apply to get started.
