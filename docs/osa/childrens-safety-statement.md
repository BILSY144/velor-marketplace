## 1. Voluntary assessment against the OSA children's harm categories

**Primary priority content (most serious — children must be prevented from
encountering it):**

| Content | Exposure risk on Velor | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Pornographic content | Negligible | Hard-blocked in listing moderation; no user image feeds; UGC images enter only via moderated listings |
| Content encouraging suicide or self-harm | Negligible | No forums/feeds today; commerce-scoped surfaces; hard rules in content policy; report + 24–48h takedown |
| Content promoting eating disorders | Negligible | As above; no relevant content surfaces |

**Priority content (children must be protected from by proportionate measures):**

| Content | Exposure risk on Velor | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Abusive or hateful content | Low | Message/chat/review filters; live-stream reporting + auto-end; takedown |
| Bullying | Low | No child-to-child surfaces; 1:1 messaging commerce-scoped, filtered, identity-masked |
| Violent content | Negligible | Weapons and violent goods hard-blocked in listings |
| Dangerous stunts/challenges | Negligible | No video UGC beyond seller product/live content, which is moderated/reportable |
| Encouraging harmful substances | Negligible | Drugs/psychoactive substances hard-blocked in listings |

Conclusion of the voluntary assessment: on current functionality the service
presents **negligible-to-low risk to any child who did access it**, and the
measures below hold that position as features grow.

## 2. Standing children's safety measures (adopted now, voluntarily)

1. **Age posture:** the service is stated 18+ in terms; transacting requires a
   payment card; seller onboarding runs regulated KYC. No highly effective age
   assurance is deployed today — if the access position changes, age assurance
   is the first measure to be evaluated, before content measures.
2. **Suspected-minor protocol:** any account reasonably suspected to belong to
   a minor is restricted pending verification, and its data handled per the
   ICO Children's Code (minimum collection, no profiling).
3. **Child-safe defaults for the social layer (binding on the Velor Social
   build, per LAW #4):** profiles private by default; no public follower
   lists at launch; no engagement-maximising nudge patterns (no streaks, no
   infinite algorithmic feed, no autoplay-next); notifications minimal and
   quiet by default; no behavioural advertising to anyone.
4. **ICO Children's Code alignment** (the applicable standards given our
   audience): best interests of the child where any child is affected; data
   minimisation; default-off geolocation sharing (Velor shares none);
   transparency in plain English; no nudge techniques — these are applied to
   ALL users, which is both simpler and stronger.
5. **Reporting:** the report and complaints routes in the Online Safety Policy
   (document 3) are open to children, parents and non-users alike; CSEA
   content is escalated immediately on sight with preservation and reporting
   to the relevant authority (in the UK, via the NCA/CEOP route).

## 3. Triggers that convert this voluntary posture into statutory duties

Any of the following triggers the full statutory children's risk assessment
within the legal window, using Ofcom's Protection of Children guidance:

- Evidence of a significant number of child users (support contacts, payment
  signals, analytics, complaints) — checked at each scheduled review;
- A new feature materially attractive to children (assessed per-feature before
  launch, per LAW #4);
- Ofcom guidance or correspondence indicating the condition is met.
