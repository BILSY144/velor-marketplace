## 2. Content rules (plain English — basis for the public-facing terms update)

Velor is a marketplace for newly made cultural and artisan goods. On any part of
Velor where users can post — listings, reviews, messages, live chat, and future
social features — the following are never allowed: illegal content of any kind
(including terrorism, child sexual abuse or exploitation, threats, harassment,
hate, fraud, sale of prohibited goods); counterfeits and scams; content
encouraging suicide or self-harm; sexual or extreme content; attempts to move
payment off Velor's protected checkout; and impersonation. Listings must comply
with the Seller Rules and Product Compliance Policy (prohibited items, regulated
materials, certificates). Breaking these rules leads to content removal and, for
serious or repeated breaches, account suspension or removal and — where legally
required — reporting to authorities.

## 3. Reporting, complaints and appeals

- **Report routes live today:** live streams (in-player reasoned report form,
  five distinct accounts auto-end a stream and open an ops review ticket);
  listings ("Report this listing" on every product page); anything else via
  customerservice@velorcommerce.co.uk (monitored inbox, available to non-users).
- **Committed before Velor Social launch (build list):**
  1. Report button on EVERY piece of user content — adding reviews and messages
     to the existing stream/listing coverage, and shipping on Collections,
     Journal posts and Feed items from day one.
  2. A public /safety (or /report) page: how to report, what happens next, and a
     form usable WITHOUT an account.
  3. An appeals route: a user whose content is removed or account is actioned can
     appeal; appeals are reviewed by a person and answered with reasons.
  4. Block and mute between users.
  5. Posting rate limits on new accounts.
- **Service levels:** illegal-content reports actioned within 24–48 hours;
  CSEA/terrorism content immediately on sight, with preservation and reporting to
  the relevant authority. All actions recorded with dates.

## 4. Moderation baseline (current machinery of record)

Automated listing moderation with hard-block categories and regulated-material
certificate gating; post-listing review; verified-purchase-only reviews; shared
content filter (lib/messageFilter) applied to messages, reviews, returns,
disputes, live titles/chat; escrow payments and payout-rail KYC; admin takedown
across all content types; AgentLog dated records; IP/counterfeit complaint route
via customerservice@ handled with takedown-first urgency.

## 5. Record-keeping and review

Risk assessments, this policy, and their revisions are version-controlled in the
service repository with dates and authorship. Moderation and takedown actions are
recorded in the operational database (AgentLog, LiveStreamReport, product status
history). This policy and both assessments are reviewed annually (next:
2027-07-29) and before every new user-to-user feature launch. Any correspondence
from Ofcom is acknowledged promptly and answered by the named owner.

## 6. EU/global posture

EU users: Velor currently falls within the DSA micro-enterprise exemption but
voluntarily maintains the substance — designated contact point (legal@
velorcommerce.co.uk), notice-and-action via the report routes above, and
moderation policy stated in terms. US: FTC endorsement and review rules respected
(no fake or incentivised-undisclosed reviews; verified-purchase gating). One
standard — the strictest — applied globally (plan §6/§8).
